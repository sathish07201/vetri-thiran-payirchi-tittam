### Evaluation Checklist for Mentor
1. **Scenario 1: Home Interior Planning**:
   - Navigate to **Home Interior** tab.
   - Adjust the budget slider (e.g. ₹60,000) and select rooms (Living Room, Bedroom).
   - Test item quantities for lights, ceiling fans, dining table, sofa, and storage.
   - Click **Re-calculate Interior Plan** to verify Gemini 3.8 Flash budget allocation across **IKEA**, **Amazon**, **Flipkart**, and **Pepperfry**.

2. **Scenario 2: AI-Based Party Budget Planning**:
   - Navigate to **Party Planner** tab.
   - Set guest count (e.g. 30 guests) and event type (Birthday or Wedding).
   - Toggle **Include OYO Guest Stay / Rooms**.
   - Check the per-person cost breakdown, timeline schedule, and vendor options from **Swiggy**, **Zomato**, and **Amazon**.

3. **Scenario 3: Jewelry Recommendations with Outfit Matching**:
   - Navigate to **Jewelry & Outfit** tab.
   - Select an occasion (e.g. Wedding / Bridal) and style (Kundan & Meenakari).
   - Choose one of the 4 sample outfits (e.g. Emerald Silk Saree or Blush Pink Lehenga) or upload a custom photo.
   - Verify that Gemini Multimodal Vision extracts dominant palette colors, neckline styles, and coordinated pieces from **Amazon**, **Flipkart**, and **Myntra**.

4. **Workspace & Epics Tracking**:
   - Navigate to **Workspace & Kanban**.
   - Check all **8 Epics** and **16 Tasks**.
   - Test moving tasks between columns (To Do, In Progress, Review, Completed).

5. **Technical Architecture Compliance**:
   - Navigate to **Tech Architecture** to inspect the real-time system requirements checker and FastAPI + Jinja template engine bridge specifications.
